## Problem: Reverse a String (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-string/

### Approach

I used two pointers, one at the beginning and one at the end.
I swapped the characters and moved the pointers toward the center.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

Tested the string "hello" and another string "abc".
The solution was accepted on LeetCode.
