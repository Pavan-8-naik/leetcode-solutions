## Problem: Valid Anagram (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/valid-anagram/

### Approach

I sorted both strings and compared them.
If both sorted strings are the same, they are anagrams.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n log n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

Tested a normal anagram case and a non-anagram case.
The solution was accepted on LeetCode.
