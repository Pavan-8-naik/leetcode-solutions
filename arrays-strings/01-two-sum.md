## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

I used two loops to check every possible pair of numbers.
When two numbers add up to the target, I return their indexes.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

Tested a normal case and a duplicate-number case.
The solution was accepted on LeetCode.
