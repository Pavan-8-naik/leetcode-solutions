## Problem: Move Zeroes (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/move-zeroes/

### Approach

I moved all non-zero elements to the beginning of the array.
Then I filled the remaining positions with zeros.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

Tested a normal case and a single-zero case.
The solution was accepted on LeetCode.
