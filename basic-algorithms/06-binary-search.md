## Problem: Binary Search (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/binary-search/

### Approach

I used two pointers, left and right, to search the sorted array.
I checked the middle element and reduced the search range based on the target.

### Complexity

- Time: O(log n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

Tested a case where the target exists and a case where the target is not present.
The solution was accepted on LeetCode.
